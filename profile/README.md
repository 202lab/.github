---

## Products

### [Zone2](https://zone2.it)

The modern endurance training and coaching platform designed for runners, track & field athletes, clubs, and coaches.

- **Workout Planning & Periodization**: Plan, schedule, and structure workouts, intervals, and training cycles.
- **Roster & Club Management**: Centralize athlete monitoring, squad communication, and team calendars in a single dashboard.
- **Activity & Results Analysis**: Track workout completion, analyze session results, and monitor group statistics and athlete progression.
- **Multi-Device Synchronization**: Direct integration with leading GPS devices and sports ecosystems (Garmin, Strava, Coros, Polar, Suunto, Apple Watch).

### [Zone2 Toolbox](https://toolbox.zone2.it)

A specialized suite of endurance calculators for athletes and coaches (VMA/paces, race time predictors, HR training zones, and FFA scoring).
